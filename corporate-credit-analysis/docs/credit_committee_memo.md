# Indicative Credit Committee Memo — INDUSTRIA SAS

**Status:** Training case / fictional borrower / preliminary review only  
**Request:** €5.0m equipment loan, seven-year amortisation  
**Purpose:** Acquisition of an industrial production line  
**Borrower:** INDUSTRIA SAS, fictional manufacturing company, 120 employees

## Executive summary
FY2025 revenue is €25.0m (+13.64% year-on-year), EBITDA €4.9m (19.6% margin), EBIT €3.5m and net income €2.2m (8.8% margin). Operating performance appears positive on the supplied simplified income statement. However, the current debt stock, balance sheet, working capital and operating cash flow are unknown.

## Proposed facility
€5.0m amortising term loan over seven years. For modelling purposes only, a fixed annual 5% rate and equal annual debt service are assumed. The facility structure and pricing remain subject to credit approval.

## Strengths
- Three years of revenue and EBITDA growth in the fictional historical data.
- Positive profitability and EBITDA margin.
- Clear productive investment purpose.

## Risks and missing evidence
- No balance sheet, debt schedule or cash-flow statement.
- No validated project economics, payback, customer contracts or supplier quotations.
- Unknown existing bank debt, guarantees, collateral, capex and working capital needs.
- No borrower rating, KYC verification or compliance review.
- Stress scenarios are illustrative and do not demonstrate actual repayment capacity.

## Recommendation
**Defer credit approval pending due diligence.** Do not infer affordability from EBITDA alone. Request verified accounts, debt schedule, cash-flow bridge and forward-looking CFADS, and rerun base/downside cases including *all* debt service. Consider a covenant package, borrower equity contribution and security only after reviewing transaction specifics.

## Next committee gate
Submit a reconciled forecast, true DSCR, leverage, liquidity, investment ROI and stress-test covenant headroom, together with the completed KYC and credit risk assessment.
