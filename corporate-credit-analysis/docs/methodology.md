## Loan
Hypothetical €5m fully amortising term loan over seven years at an assumed fixed 5% annual rate. Constant annual payments, end-of-year, no fees, no grace period. This is an exercise assumption, not an actual offer.

## Scenarios
Base: +4% annual revenue growth, 19.6% EBITDA margin.
Downside: -2% annual revenue growth, 15% EBITDA margin.
Severe: -8% annual revenue growth, 11% EBITDA margin.
These are simplified illustrative constant-growth paths, **not forecasts validated by management**.

## Critical distinction: EBITDA/debt-service is NOT DSCR
The model displays EBITDA divided by **new loan** debt service, a rough screening proxy. True cash flow available for debt service (CFADS) must account for cash taxes, working capital movements, maintenance capex and other relevant cash items; the denominator must include all applicable scheduled debt service, including existing borrowings. No genuine DSCR, net debt/EBITDA, liquidity, ROE, ROCE, DSO, DPO or DIO can be computed without additional balance sheet and cash flow information.

## Underwriting checklist
Obtain audited financial statements and annexes; current debt and leasing schedule; monthly cash flow forecast; existing security and covenants; investment budget and supplier quotes; equity contribution; collateral valuations; customer and supplier concentrations; credit bureau and payment incidents; group structure and guarantees; KYC/AML and beneficial ownership. Rebuild CFADS and total debt service before a decision.

## Possible extension
Create a fully reconciled balance sheet and cash flow statement, calculate BFR and working-capital days, project free cash flow, build sensitivities and a covenant headroom dashboard, then draft a final decision.
